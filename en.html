Quality control

Global standard

Kyungwon has built a system that meets strict global standards and manages quality and lead time thoroughly. Step-by-step inspection lets us solve problems quickly and meet our customers’ quality requirements.

  1. STEP 01Yarn check

    Incoming yarn is compared and matched against the list, and stacked according to the production procedure.

  2. STEP 02Yarn quality inspection

    Incoming yarn is inspected and recorded; yarn with defects is kept separately with the reason noted.

  3. STEP 03PP sample check

    For the sample confirmed by the buyer, design, colour and size are examined in detail.

  4. STEP 04Confirmation before production

    The person in charge and the QC team review potential issues and key points for the style before production starts.

  5. STEP 05Initial product inspection

    Initial products are checked in a set quantity during production to confirm the line is running without problems.

KYUNGWON QMS Quality Management System

Kyungwon practises a QMS quality policy. Each unit sets measurable quality objectives and puts them into practice and review. Through continuous implementation and improvement we work to produce products anyone can trust and choose.

Knitting QC

Checks colour and design against the P.P sample confirmed by the buyer, along with overall appearance, defects, loose yarn and size.

Linking QC

Checks both outside and inside for cutting, joining, linking and thread finishing.

Finishing QC

Checks bottom, ankle, rib and body size, overall packing condition, labels and stickers, and C/T box marking and quantities.

FacilityIn-house sample room

Samples are knitted immediately to a buyer’s request, so new designs develop quickly. Developers with deep understanding of socks work with KW Lab on new materials, patterns and structures.

Patent No. 10-1752712

Hanji socks and their manufacturing method

Socks made by twisting spandex with Hanji yarn from the bark of the paper mulberry. Hanji’s natural antibacterial and deodorising properties reduce odour, it absorbs moisture well and dries fast. Far-infrared emission and good air flow help keep feet healthy, and the socks are cool in summer and warm in winter.
